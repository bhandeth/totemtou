The four standards

What every Totemtou journey delivers.

I · Leadership

A leader who has walked it

Every group has a leader who has done the route, repeatedly, in the conditions we're going in. They walk what we publish.

II · Routes

Walked before published

We publish only what we've tested. Every route has been walked end to end by a founder.

III · Logistics

The country, managed

Permits, stays, transport, food, contingencies. The traveller doesn't manage the country; the country has been managed for them.

IV · Scale

Small enough to know names

The group moves at its actual pace. Small enough that the leader knows everyone's name on day one.

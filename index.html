The three ways to journey

Different people want different relationships with the land.

Every Totemtou journey lives inside one of three ways. Pick the relationship; we'll point you at the journeys.

i.

Walk the Land

High-altitude trekking. The day is simple: walk, breathe, eat, rest, repeat.
ii.

Traverse the Land

4×4 expeditions through remote terrain. Not road trips — terrain planning, cultural respect, serious logistics.
iii.

Flow with the Land

Cycling and expedition MTB. Movement as meditation when the trail demands presence.
The four standards

What every Totemtou journey delivers.

Regardless of way, region, or duration, every journey we run holds these four lines. They are non-negotiable.

Every group has a leader who has done the route, repeatedly, in the conditions we're going in. Not a guide, not a chaperone, but a leader. They walk what we publish, and we publish only what we've tested. Every route has been walked end to end by a founder.

Permits, stays, transport, food, contingencies. The traveller doesn't manage the country; the country has been managed for them. The group is small enough to move at its actual pace, not the slowest published one. Small enough that the leader knows everyone's name on day one.

These are the lines we hold. They cost what they cost.
